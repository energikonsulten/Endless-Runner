Privacy Policy — Cyber Runner (Endless Runner)

Last updated: 7 September 2026

App: Cyber Runner (Endless Runner)

Package name: com.jimpa.endlessrunner

Platform: Android (Godot 4)

This privacy policy describes how Cyber Runner handles information in its current release configuration. It is written from the app’s actual behavior today. It is not legal advice.

Summary (current build)

  • The app is designed to run **without network access**.
  • **Live advertising is not enabled.** Ads use a local mock mode only.
  • **No analytics** are documented or described as part of this build.
  • We do **not** operate user accounts in the app as described for this build.
  • We do **not** intentionally collect personal information from your device over the internet in this configuration.

Who we are

Cyber Runner is an Android endless-runner game published under the package name com.jimpa.endlessrunner.

For privacy questions about this app, contact the developer through the contact details listed on the Google Play store listing for Cyber Runner (or the email address published with the listing when available).

What the app does with data today

Offline / no network permission

In the current export configuration, Android network permissions used for online features (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) are not enabled. The app is not set up to phone home, load remote ads, or send analytics in this state.

Advertising

Advertising is currently in mock mode (use_mock=true). That means ad slots are local placeholders for development/testing. Third-party ad networks are not live in this configuration, so the app is not using those networks to collect or share advertising identifiers or ad-related personal data.

When live ads are turned on in a future update, this policy and the Google Play Data safety answers will be updated before that change ships.

Analytics and crash reporting

No analytics SDK or crash-reporting service is documented for the current build. This policy does not claim that any such service is active.

Information that may stay on your device

Like most games, Cyber Runner may store game-related information on your device only (for example progress, settings, or similar local game state), using the device’s normal app storage. In the current no-network configuration, that information is not sent by the app to our servers or to third parties over the internet, because the app is not configured for network access.

We do not treat ordinary local game save/settings data as “collected” by us for Play Data safety purposes while it remains only on the device and is not transmitted off the device by the app.

Permissions

Beyond what Android and Godot require for a normal local game, the current export turns network-related permissions off until live ads (or other online features) are intentionally enabled.

Children

Cyber Runner is a general-audience endless runner. The current build does not include age-gated features, parental controls, or a child-directed experience design that we document here. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us using the Play listing contact details.

*(Uncertainty flag: we have not completed a formal children’s-privacy legal review. Do not treat this section as a COPPA/GDPR-kids determination.)*

Data security

Because the current build is configured without network access for ads/analytics, there is no app-operated transmission of personal data to our servers to secure in transit. Local data is protected by the device’s normal Android app sandbox and whatever security settings you use on your device.

Your choices

  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes the app and its local on-device data (subject to how Android handles backups on your device).
  • There is no in-app account deletion flow documented for this build, because the app does not document user accounts or remotely stored player profiles.

Changes to this policy

If we enable live ads, analytics, accounts, or other online features, we will update this privacy policy and the Google Play Data safety form to match the real behavior before relying on those features in a published build.

Contact

Use the developer contact method shown on the Cyber Runner Google Play listing.
